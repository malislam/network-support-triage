This table summarizes equipment and work confirmed in my resume and career notes. It describes my experience, while the runbooks and incident examples in this repository are newly authored demonstrations. The table does not imply work on Voxel equipment or production Linux/IoT systems.

| --- | --- | --- |

| HP ProLiant BL460c, VMware vCenter, EMC VNX/Unity | Supporting server and virtualization infrastructure at Lincoln Center | Recognize the server, virtual-network, and storage teams involved when network checks do not explain a service fault |

## Boundaries I would state in an interview

- I can discuss the actual networking work and the reasoning behind these examples.
- I would learn the product's device architecture, logs, and escalation procedures before claiming platform expertise.
- The VMware/storage entry does not establish ownership of every storage or hypervisor function.
- A restore of connectivity is not automatically a diagnosis of the underlying cause.
